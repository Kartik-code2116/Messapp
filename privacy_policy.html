Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

Welcome to MessApp. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless experience for managing mess subscriptions and meal plans, we collect the following information:

  • Personal Identity Information: Name, Email Address, and Phone Number provided during account registration.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Tokens: Used to send push notifications regarding daily menu updates, subscription statuses, and alerts from mess owners.
  • Payment & Transaction Information: All subscription payments processed in this app are simulated for testing and demonstration purposes. No real money or actual credit/debit card information is processed or stored. Actual payment settlements are handled offline directly between the user and the mess owner.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users and manage active accounts.
  • To track meal attendance ("IN" or "OUT") and manage monthly subscription statuses.
  • To deliver real-time push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • To provide analytics to mess owners regarding active subscriptions and revenue metrics.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement secure Firebase rules and industry-standard security protocols to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or deletion of your personal data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties.

4. Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. To request deletion:

  • Email the developer directly at kartikthorat2116@gmail.com using your registered email address.
  • Once requested, we will delete your authentication profile, personal registration data (name, email, phone number), and active meal selections.
  • Your data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 7 business days, with no additional data retention period.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at kartikthorat2116@gmail.com or your mess owner.
